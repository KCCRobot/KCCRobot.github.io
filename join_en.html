Membership Application Guide

Join KCCR to expand the care robotics ecosystem and pioneer the future together.

Membership Categories & Benefits

🤝 Regular Member (Corp / Indiv)

  • Eligibility: Robotics manufacturers, component makers, and academic/clinical researchers
  • Investment Standard:
    • Corporate: KRW 2,000,000 (20 shares)+
    • Individual: KRW 200,000 (2 shares)+
  • Key Benefits:
    • Voting rights in the General Assembly
    • Participation in joint R&D and demonstration projects
    • Discounts on workshops, conferences, and exhibition booths

🌟 Supporting Member

  • Eligibility: Organizations or individuals supporting KCCR's goals
  • Voting Status: Non-voting (No general assembly voting rights)
  • Key Benefits:
    • Invitations and discounts for official events
    • Access to policy reports and technical papers
    • Promotion through official KCCR media channels
