Overcoming the Super-Aged Society Care Crisis
Through Robotic Innovation and Global Solidarity

Welcome to the Korea Co-op of Care Robots (KCCR).
I am Kyung-Hwan Kim, the 2nd President of KCCR.

South Korea is entering a super-aged society at an unprecedented speed worldwide, facing urgent and complex caregiving challenges for seniors and persons with disabilities across all sectors of society. Continuing with traditional caregiving models and labor-intensive approaches poses unsustainable burdens on national welfare systems while leaving individuals and families without viable solutions.

To address this critical challenge, KCCR was established in July 2022 by visionary researchers, innovators, and corporations dedicated to the research and development of robotics. While robotics alone cannot solve every dimension of caregiving, our cooperative is firmly committed to developing common core technologies, supporting commercialization, and actively contributing to national policy agendas to deliver practical and impactful solutions.

Furthermore, we will actively expand global alliances and strategic partnerships with nations facing similar demographic transitions, including Japan and international partners across the globe.

We cordially invite researchers, industry leaders, healthcare professionals, and all of you to join and support us in building a sustainable and compassionate caregiving ecosystem.

Key Initiatives & Core Capabilities

  • Collaborative R&D & Core Technology: Building shared technical convergence platforms connecting care robot manufacturers, medical specialists, and rehabilitation engineers.
  • Field Testing & Commercialization: Operating user-centric clinical testbeds and advancing safety and quality standardization for care robotics.
  • Policy Advocacy & Institutional Support: Proactively proposing policy agendas, including long-term care insurance benefit item registration and public support frameworks.
  • Global Alliances & International Networking: Fostering joint research, technology exchange, and cross-border cooperation with Japan and global robotics networks.
