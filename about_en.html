About KCCR & CI

Introducing the vision, history, organizational structure, and key initiatives of KCCR.

1. Mission & CI

Korea Co-op of Care Robots (KCCR) was established to advance welfare robotics and improve the national caregiving infrastructure through autonomous, cooperative efforts.

Core Objectives & Background

  • Addressing Super-Aged Societies: Mitigating heavy physical workloads on healthcare workers and improving recipients' quality of life.
  • Joint R&D & Commercialization: Fostering collaboration among academia, research institutes, hospitals, and enterprises to develop and deploy field-tailored care robots.
  • Industrial Ecosystem Development: Standardizing core modular components (omni-wheels, AI vision, HRI) and conducting joint marketing.

KCCR Visual Identity (CI)

The official symbol represents human-centered Care, advanced Robotics, and multidisciplinary Cooperation.

4. Major Initiatives

Night-time Nursing Rounding Robots

  • Omni-directional mobility in narrow ward environments with smooth S-curve control
  • Low-light IR and 3D ToF vision fusion for patient monitoring and fall detection
  • On-device voice AI and non-contact vital sign monitoring integrated with EMR/OCS

Transfer & Mobility Assistance

  • Smart power wheelchairs, standing & transfer assist devices, and walking gait trainers
  • Automated toileting and shower-assist robotic capsules
  • Standardized lightweight omni-wheel drive modules

Joint Marketing & Consulting

  • Cooperative brand promotion and joint pavilions at global robotics exhibitions
  • Clinical validation testbeds (PoC) at tertiary hospitals and nursing homes
  • Smart hospital process innovation consulting
